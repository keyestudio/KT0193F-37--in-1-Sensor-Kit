### Project 15: Line Tracking Sensor
![image204](media/ef0c045b145fa941f18b0bbdbd430726.png)

**Description**

![image205](media/f8c7f40032c689fa177566fb5a754ad5.png) This Line Tracking Sensor can detect white lines on a black background or black lines on a white background. The single line-tracking signal provides a stable TTL output signal for more accurate and stable line detection. A multi-channel option can be easily achieved by installing the required line-tracking robot sensors.

| Operating Voltage           | 3.3V ~ 5V    |
|-----------------------------|--------------|
| Operating current           | <10mA       |
| Operating temperature range | 0°C ~ + 50°C |
| Output Level                | TTL level    |

**Specification**

**How Does It Work?**

The KY-033 module detects whether the surface in front of the IR transmitter/receiver absorbs or reflects light. A dark surface absorbs light, while a light surface reflects light. If the surface is dark, the module's Signal pin will be pulled HIGH. If the surface is light, the signal pin will be LOW.

Pinout

![image207](media/d60f560a54c26743e6bab7e4be0ccc56.png)

SIN is the line-tracking sensor signal pin which we connect to the Arduino.

VCC should be connected to the +5V Power of Arduino.

GND should be connected to the ground of Arduino.

**Connection Diagram**

Connect the module’s GND line (the leftmost pin) to GND on the Arduino and + (the second pin) to 5V.

Connect signal (out) to pin 3 on the Arduino.
