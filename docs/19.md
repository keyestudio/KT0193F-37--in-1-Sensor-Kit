### Project 19: Soil Humidity Sensor
![image249](media/bb1c81e52c5e8999e4aba7a9c2b29f5d.png)

**Description**

![image250](media/534ec357118ab110fff66a77bad695d0.png) This is a simple soil humidity sensor designed to detect soil humidity. If the soil lacks water, the analog value output by the sensor will decrease; otherwise, it will increase.

If you use this sensor to create an automatic watering device, it can detect whether your plants are thirsty, preventing them from wilting when you are away.

Combining this sensor with an Arduino controller can make your plants more comfortable and your garden smarter.

This soil humidity sensor module is not as complicated as you might think, making it an excellent choice if you need to detect soil moisture in your project.

 The sensor features two probes that are inserted into the soil. When current flows through the soil, the sensor measures the resistance by detecting changes in current between the two probes, then converts this resistance value into moisture content.

 The higher the moisture (less resistance), the higher the soil's conductivity.

 The sensor's surface has undergone a metallization process to extend its service life. Insert it into the soil and use an AD converter to read its values. With this sensor, your plants can remind you: I need water.

**Specification**

1.  Power Supply Voltage: 3.3V or 5V
2.  Working Current: ≤ 20mA
3.  Output Voltage: 0-2.3V (When the sensor is fully immersed in water, the voltage will be 2.3V)
4.  The higher the humidity, the higher the output voltage.
5.  Sensor type: Analog output
6.  Interface: Pin1- signal, Pin2- GND, Pin3 - VCC

**How Does It Work?**

 The operation of the soil moisture sensor is quite straightforward.

 The fork-shaped probe, with two exposed conductors, acts as a **variable resistor** (much like a potentiometer) whose resistance changes according to the water content in the soil.

![image251](media/b89d46a0abbeb3eb175c5f93bc58efcb.GIF)

 This resistance is inversely proportional to the soil moisture:

 More water in the soil means better conductivity and will result in lower resistance.

 Less water in the soil means poor conductivity and will result in higher resistance.

 The sensor produces an output voltage based on this resistance, which we can measure to determine the moisture level.

**Pinout**

![image252](media/1c4d0626fb34f06467f2b6aa49512e2c.png)

SIN is the soil humidity sensor signal pin which we connect to the Arduino.

VCC should be connected to the +5V Power of Arduino.

GND should be connected to the ground of Arduino.

**Connection Diagram**

Connect the S pin of the module to Analog A0 of the PLUS board, connect the negative pin to the GND port, and the positive pin to the 5V port.
