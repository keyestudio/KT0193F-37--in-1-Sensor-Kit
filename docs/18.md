### Project 18: Water Level Sensor
![image240](media/48fca6f54c4deedf6fb8a39e3ac3c7b5.png)

**Description**

![image241](media/1b841884827f3de5d72b26bb6f1332f2.png) The water level sensor is easy-to-use, portable, and cost-effective, designed to identify and detect water levels and water drops.

This small sensor can measure the volume of a water drop or water quantity through an array of traces of exposed parallel wires.

**Features**

- Smooth conversion between water quantity and analog quantity;
- Strong flexibility, outputting basic analog values;
- Low power consumption and high sensitivity;
- Directly connects to a microprocessor or other logic circuits, suitable for a variety of development boards and controllers such as the Arduino controller, STC single-chip microcomputer, AVR single-chip microcomputer, and more.

**How Does It Work?**

The working of the water level sensor is pretty straightforward.

The series of exposed parallel conductors together acts as a **variable resistor** (just like a potentiometer) whose resistance varies according to the water level.

The change in resistance corresponds to the distance from the top of the sensor to the surface of the water.

The resistance is inversely proportional to the height of the water:

The more water the sensor is immersed in, the better the conductivity, which will result in a lower resistance.

![image242](media/c392a2d336c21fb7bb74786ba1b4cb37.GIF)

The less water the sensor is immersed in, the poorer the conductivity, which will

result in a higher resistance.

The sensor produces an output voltage according to the resistance, which by measuring we can determine the water level.

SIN is the water level sensor signal pin which we connect to the Arduino.

VCC should be connected to the +5V Power of Arduino.

GND should be connected to the ground of Arduino.

Furthermore, you can set an alarm value and connect a buzzer to make an alarm.

The LED can’t light up when the water level hasn’t reached the alarm value. If the water level reaches the alarm value, the LED will be turned on and the buzzer will sound to make an alarm.
